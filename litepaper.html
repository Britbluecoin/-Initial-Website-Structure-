BritBlue Litepaper.

A plain-English overview of what the project is, how it intends to launch and the standards it is setting for transparency.

1. Purpose

BritBlue is a cat-focused community project inspired by Barnaby, Jojo and the memory of Buffy. Its purpose is to combine entertainment, community participation and a measurable cat-welfare mission.

2. Network and launch

BritBlue is planned for the Solana network with a public launch through Pump.fun. The final contract address will only be considered official once it is published on this website and mirrored through BritBlue’s official social accounts.

3. Distribution philosophy

The project intends to avoid opaque private allocations. Launch mechanics, founder participation, charity-related wallets and development or marketing funding will be disclosed before they are treated as final. Any figures discussed before that disclosure are planning assumptions, not guarantees.

4. Charity mission

A meaningful portion of project value is intended to support cat welfare. The exact mechanism may include designated wallets and/or a share of project-generated creator revenue. Confirmed percentages and wallet addresses will be published when operational.

5. Transparency

BritBlue intends to publish regular summaries of charity activity, material project spending and significant milestones. Where on-chain records are relevant, the website will provide enough information for community members to inspect them independently.

6. Brand and community

Barnaby and Jojo are not decorative mascots; they are the ongoing narrative engine of BritBlue. Comics, videos, character stories and community humour are intended to create a project people follow because they enjoy it, not solely because of price speculation.

7. Risks

Cryptocurrency is highly speculative and can lose most or all of its value. Smart-contract, platform, liquidity, regulatory, market and operational risks may affect the project. BritBlue makes no promise of profit, price appreciation or liquidity.

8. No financial advice

Nothing on the BritBlue website or official social channels should be interpreted as investment, legal, tax or financial advice. Prospective participants should make their own decisions and only risk funds they can afford to lose.

Pre-launch document: This website edition is intentionally conservative. Final token-specific figures, addresses and launch details will be inserted only after they are verified.
