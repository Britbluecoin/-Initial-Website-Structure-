Frequently asked questions.

The short version of the important stuff — including what is not final yet.

What is BritBlue?

BritBlue is a cat-themed community project inspired by Barnaby, Jojo and the memory of Buffy, with a stated mission to support cat welfare through a transparent project model.

Has the token launched?

No. BritBlue is currently pre-launch. Do not trust a contract address unless it is published on this website and official BritBlue channels.

Which blockchain is planned?

Solana is the planned network, with Pump.fun as the intended public launch venue.

Who are Barnaby and Jojo?

Barnaby is the older British Blue and the project’s founder voice. Jojo is his younger sister and co-star. Their real personalities shape the project’s stories and humour.

Who is Buffy?

Buffy was Barnaby’s sister. Her memory inspired the project’s cat-welfare mission and the line “For Buffy. For Every Cat.”

How will charity support work?

The project intends to use publicly disclosed mechanisms such as designated wallets and/or project-generated revenue. Final percentages, addresses and distribution rules will be published when operational.

Is BritBlue an investment?

BritBlue is a speculative cryptocurrency project. Nothing on the website is financial advice and there is no guarantee of profit, liquidity or continued value.

How do I avoid scams?

Use only links published on this website or the official social accounts. Ignore unsolicited DMs, “presale” offers, wallet-connection requests and contract addresses posted by third parties.
