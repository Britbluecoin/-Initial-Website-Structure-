12-month voluntary holding commitments

The stated holding period is 7 October 2026 to 7 October 2027. Approximately 270.62 million BritBlue are designated for voluntary holding. The Marketing and Development wallets each designate 95% for holding, with a combined 4.75 million BritBlue operational reserve. All other listed wallets designate 100% of their reported allocations.

Important: These are voluntary commitments, not blockchain-enforced locks. Wallet controllers retain technical control and can transfer tokens. The addresses alone do not prove ownership or adherence; community members may review on-chain movements.

Charity treasury and creator fees

The reported Charity allocation is 70 million BritBlue, intended to remain held for the initial commitment period. Initial cat-welfare donations are intended to be funded using Pump.fun creator fees actually received and available. Planned donations will not be described as completed until they occur. Recipient information and evidence will be shared where appropriate and permitted.

Reporting

BritBlue intends to publish monthly updates on material wallet movements, creator fees received, completed donations and marketing or development spending. Significant changes to commitments should be disclosed promptly. Publication is not an independent financial or blockchain audit.

Risks

BritBlue is a high-risk cryptocurrency. Voluntary holding commitments do not guarantee market liquidity, token value, or charitable results. Token purchases are not automatically tax-deductible donations. Never share wallet seed phrases or private keys.
