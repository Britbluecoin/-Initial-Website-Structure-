Meet the family.

BritBlue is not built around a generic mascot. Barnaby, Jojo and Buffy are the reason the project has a personality, a story and a purpose.

B

Barnaby

The older brother: serious, food-motivated, dignified and mildly suspicious of unnecessary enthusiasm. Barnaby is the founder voice of BritBlue and wears the project’s signature gold tag.

J

Jojo

Barnaby’s younger sister: smaller, bright-eyed, cuddly and quietly confident. Jojo gives BritBlue a playful counterweight to Barnaby’s dry seriousness.

♥

Buffy

Barnaby’s late sister and the inspiration behind the charity mission. BritBlue is designed to keep her memory connected to practical help for cats in need.

Voice & character

Humour with a serious centre.

Barnaby can complain about dinner being late, demand milk, inspect the house and endure Jojo’s teasing. But when BritBlue talks about welfare, charity or transparency, the tone becomes clear and sincere.

That mix matters. The project should be enjoyable enough to follow every day without losing sight of why it exists.

What BritBlue is trying to build

BritBlue aims to become a recognisable cat-focused community that can produce stories, comics, short films and social content while maintaining a visible welfare mission. The brand is intended to outlive any single market cycle by giving people characters and a cause worth caring about.
